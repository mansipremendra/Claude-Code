Privacy

VeritasHire is a division of Growketing Ads Private Limited.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in our booking form: your name, work email and team size.
  • Website analytics, collected through Google Analytics 4, such as pages viewed, scroll depth and approximate location.
  • Advertising measurement data, collected through the Meta Pixel, such as whether a visit led to a form submission or a booked call.
  • Recordings and notes of supervision meetings held during a 90-day term. Meetings are recorded with everyone's consent.

Why we collect it

  • To prepare for and run your call, and to reply to you.
  • To understand how the website is used and to measure our advertising.
  • To supervise placements and keep accountability during the 90-day term.

Booking calendar

Bookings are made through Calendly. Details you enter there are handled by Calendly under its own terms.

Contact

For questions about this page or your information, contact us at the email address listed on our homepage.
