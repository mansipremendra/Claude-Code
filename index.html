Questions.

What roles can you place?
Any role with defined, recurring tasks that can be managed remotely. We confirm fit on the call.
What does the monthly fee include?
The VA's salary and our management fee. The first month is paid up front, after we confirm your requirements on the consultation call and before we start vetting.
How does the buyout work?
The 90-day term is committed. Between Day 80 and Day 90 you pay a one-time $2,000 buyout and your VA moves onto your direct payroll, with no further fees to us. You can buy out earlier, from Day 45, once we have supervised for at least 45 days. An early buyout ends the replacement guarantee. After buyout your VA's pay rises to the full market rate for their role, because they are on your payroll and not ours. During the 90-day term they are on a probationary pay structure with us.
What if the VA doesn't work out?
One replacement is included within the 90-day term. We act within 5 business days. The guarantee applies to clients committed to the full 90 days and ends if you buy out early.
What if none of the three candidates suits me?
We find you new candidates at no extra charge. We confirm your requirements on the consultation call before month one is paid, so the shortlist is built around them.
Are my meetings recorded?
Yes, with everyone's consent. After the first 14 days, Fathom AI notes from your daily meetings go straight to us, so supervision and accountability do not depend on anyone's summary.
Is my business data safe?
We do not share your business data with anyone else. You decide which accounts and tools your VA can access, and you can remove that access at any time. Every VA signs a confidentiality agreement before starting. Our engagements are governed by Indian law.
How does timezone overlap work?
The UAE is 1.5 hours behind India, so your VA works your normal day. UK clients share an afternoon window. We agree working hours before the hire starts.
